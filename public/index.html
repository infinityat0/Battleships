      How to play
      • Each side hides five ships: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3) and Destroyer (2).
      • Click a square in enemy waters to fire. You and the computer take turns, one shot each.
      • When a ship is sunk it is revealed to the other side.
      • Sink all five enemy ships to win. Don't like your layout? Reshuffle before your first shot.
      • Keyboard: arrow keys aim, Enter or Space fires.
